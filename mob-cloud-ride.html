Every Litre Counts

Since the petrol subsidy was removed in 2023, fuel has become one of the biggest daily costs for Nigerian drivers, dispatch riders and keke operators. Prices swing, queues return, and engines need constant servicing.

Every naira spent on fuel is a naira not earned.

Rising fuel costs

Daily fuel spend eats into every trip, every delivery and every fare.

Hours lost in queues

Scarcity means waiting in line instead of working.

Constant servicing

Oil changes, filters and engine repairs add up over the year.

Meet Cloud Ride

Since 2013, Cloud Energy has powered homes, schools and businesses across Nigeria with solar panels, inverters and lithium storage. Cloud Ride takes that same clean power onto the road.

  • No fuel queuesCharge at home, at work or at a Cloud Energy charging point.
  • Charge from the sunPair your vehicle with a Cloud Energy solar and battery system.
  • Fewer moving partsElectric motors need no oil changes, spark plugs or exhaust repairs.
