About the 6.5kW / 48V Hybrid Inverter

The Cloud Energy 6.5kW / 48V Hybrid Inverter runs your home or business on solar, grid and battery power from a single unit. Its 120A MPPT charger accepts up to 9000W of solar across an MPPT PV voltage range of 60 – 450V (500V max), so your panels keep charging your batteries efficiently through the day.

A wide 90 – 280V AC input keeps it working on unstable grid supply, and 12kVA surge power handles start-up loads. Inbuilt Wi-Fi lets you monitor the system remotely, and parallel connection of up to 6 units lets your system grow with your energy needs.
